IThe story

Born from love at first sight on a trip to Argentina. Raised in a quiet green corner of Tirana, near the Dinamo Stadium, where the only thing allowed to be loud is the fire.

Chef Sergio Nallino brought ten years of experience and his grandmother's recipes from Argentina. Sommelier and co-founder Fation Tila chose every label in the cellar. The asado does the rest. It has always been a reason to sit together, family and friends, with a good glass of Malbec.

A steak on a real parrilla takes about forty minutes.

Start with the empanadas. That is what the wait is for.

We cook over wood and let the meat rest before it reaches you. Nothing on the grill is hurried, and nothing arrives at the table before it is ready.

    IVLa cantina · the cellar

    Beneath the restaurant runs a refuge-tunnel from the dictatorship years. We filled it with wine.

    The dictatorship left a refuge under the dining room with the exact conditions wine wants. It now holds the whole collection, and sommelier and co-founder Fation Tila chose every label in it.

    80 labels you cannot pour anywhere else in Albania. The restaurant brings them in itself from Argentina, Chile and Uruguay, and keeps a hundred and fifty more beside them.

    When you come, ask for Fation. He will walk you down the tunnel and choose the bottle with you.
